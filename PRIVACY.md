# Privacy

HandMirror shows your local camera feed in a small floating mirror window.

- Camera video stays on your device.
- HandMirror does not record camera video.
- HandMirror does not send camera video, images, or telemetry to a server.
- The camera stream starts when the mirror opens and stops when the mirror closes.
- The app needs camera permission only to show the live preview.

If your operating system shows a camera indicator, that indicator should be visible while the mirror is open.
